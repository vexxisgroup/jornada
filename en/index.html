Overview

A gathering dedicated to excellence in women's intimate health

Medicine focused on women's intimate health and regenerative therapies is undergoing a period of major scientific and technological evolution. New techniques, minimally invasive approaches, and therapeutic resources have expanded the possibilities of care, providing greater quality of life, functionality, self-esteem, and well-being for women.

With the purpose of fostering scientific updates and the exchange of knowledge among specialists, Hospital Mãe de Deus presents the International Symposium on Intimate Surgery and Regenerative Therapy in Women — a gathering that brings together some of the leading national and international names in the field to share experience, scientific evidence, technological advances, and the challenges of clinical and surgical practice.

Throughout the program, topics will include female intimate surgery, regenerative therapies, technologies applied to women's health, and trends transforming specialized care — including live surgery coverage, held in person at Hospital Mãe de Deus.

Objective

To promote the qualification and continuing education of physicians and healthcare professionals who work or wish to work in the field of female intimate surgery and regenerative therapy, through the sharing of clinical experience, discussion of scientific evidence, and presentation of the latest innovations and technologies applied to women's health.

The symposium aims to foster medical excellence, the exchange of knowledge among specialists, and the development of an increasingly safe, ethical, and evidence-based practice.

Confirmed Speakers

World references coming together in Porto Alegre

Founder and medical director of the Cellular Medicine Association (CMA), creator of the O-Shot®, P-Shot®, Vampire Facial®, and Vampire Facelift®. More than 30 years of clinical, scientific, and educational work in regenerative medicine.

United States

United States

Speakers from Brazil

JB
Host · Founder

Dr. João Brito Jaenisch Neto

Physician graduated from the Federal University of Health Sciences of Porto Alegre (UFCSPA), with a specialization in General Surgery and additional training in Vaginal Surgery in Italy and Gynecological Laparoscopy in Germany, France, and the United States. He is a national reference in female intimate surgery and regenerative and functional gynecology, having been one of the pioneers in introducing regenerative therapies and advanced technologies for women's intimate health in Brazil. Senior member of the World Academy of Regenerative & Aesthetic Gynecology (WARAG) and the Cellular Medicine Association (CMA).

PG
Event Coordinator

Dr. Paulo Guimarães

Physician with notable work in regenerative gynecology, female intimate surgery, and technologies applied to women's health. He is active in training professionals and disseminating innovative practices focused on functionality, well-being, and women's quality of life, and is recognized for his contribution to the development of the specialty in Brazil.

PP

Dr. Paulo Palmas

Full professor at Unicamp, with expertise in urogynecology and co-creator, together with Dr. Ariel Luksenburg, of the System Plus for urinary incontinence. A national reference in regenerative gynecology and female intimate aesthetics, he carries out clinical, scientific, and educational activities focused on applying new technologies and minimally invasive therapies for women's health, contributing to the continuous training of physicians and professionals in the field.

FS

Dr. Fernando Spengler

Pharmacist specializing in allopathic compounding, focused on formulations and products developed to complement and enhance surgical procedures in regenerative and functional gynecology, including healing agents, re-epithelializing agents, depigmenting agents, and drug-delivery systems.
